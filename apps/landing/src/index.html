01 / The routing desk

Your client keeps its familiar API.

Give your models
a forwarding address.

Point your OpenAI-compatible client at Alnair. A model prefix picks the upstream. A named combo gives you an ordered chain for multi-provider fallback.

Use /v1/chat/completions or /v1/responses for OpenAI clients, and /v1/messages for Anthropic clients. Keep your own provider accounts.

03 / Put it on your desk

Your machine.
Your model stack.

Install the router, open the local dashboard, and connect your first provider.

Alnair is MIT licensed. You bring your provider accounts and pay their usage costs.

Download a release binary
Shell 
npm install -g @xflawlessdev/alnair-router

Run alnair-router start to launch the router. To also enable auto-start with your session, run alnair-router install.

Next stop: your dashboard

Run alnair-router start, then open:

http://127.0.0.1:7878/login

Use the setup code from the router log to choose your dashboard password. Then add an upstream connection.
